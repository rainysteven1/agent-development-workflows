# Plotting

## Overview

Build figures around the claim they need to support. A research figure should make the main conclusion legible at a glance and the details inspectable on a second pass.

## Workflow

1. Lock the figure claim.
2. Choose the chart form that best exposes it.
3. Apply a consistent visual system across the paper or report.
4. Make units, labels, legends, and uncertainty readable.
5. Export in the format the destination requires, preferring vector output for papers.

## Constraints

- include units where relevant
- start bar charts at zero unless there is a strong reason not to
- show uncertainty for measured results when appropriate
- do not overload one figure with multiple unrelated conclusions

## Handoff

- to `paper-craft` when the figure belongs in the paper pipeline
- to `slidecraft` when the figure becomes a slide asset
